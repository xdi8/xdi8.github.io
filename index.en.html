The language of an individual

Shidinn (Xdi8 aho) is folk linguist Huang Quefei’s attempt to reimagine Chinese in an alphabetic script. For thirty years, the language drifted in a sorrowful life along with its creator. Even now, we are still heading for the unknown.

How does it work?

For the “deprecation of homophonic characters” (as the creator put it), Shidinn echoes the composition of Chinese characters with 45 phono-semantic letters, giving every character a definite “word sound” — so that Chinese, too, can be written, typed, read and learned like an alphabetic script.

To go deeper, read About Shidinn, or visit the Shidinn Wiki.

Open skies

The architecture of Shidinn never ceases, nor is it confined to a single field. Your contribution will draw wide attention. Fonts, translators, input methods, the wiki — the whole toolkit grows in the open-source community, open to every conlanger, language lover and learner.
